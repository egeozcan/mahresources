Design decisions the lane made (review them, push back if wrong):

- A stopped Job's average is its reported count, taken as its own work from zero, divided by banked running time (jobs.Snapshot.AverageRate); nil while running and when nothing was counted. - HLS: the queue resets its byte counters once the body is recognised as a playlist, hls reports bytes received and counts segments across video and audio, the mux reports every segment done, the projection counts segments first; the assembled result is mirrored.
- A finished export's final progress is its archive's size, written with the Finish; while running the estimate is a total only until the count passes it.
- Vocabulary in server/jobview/job_vocabulary.json, read by Go and JS; API values and keys are unchanged, only labels. A new API field `blockedReason` (latest blocked event's reason) on list and detail.
- One CSS class per state tone (job-tone--* in public/index.css) on the drawer, /jobs and the Job page.
- Each drawer row says how long ago its Job entered its state ("failed 3 min ago"), outside any live region, counted on the drawer's existing clock.
- A rate too slow to show per second is shown per minute or per hour.
- When a Job finishes, the trailing points of its series that counted nothing since the one before drop their rate (endAtLastMovement), so a graph ends at its last movement.
- Scheduled drawer rows are ordered client-side; with more than 50 active Jobs the soonest may be outside the drawer's read (documented known limit).
